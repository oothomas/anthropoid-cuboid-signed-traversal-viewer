Fig 1. Interactive principal-component traversals of anthropoid cuboids. The seed-2071 model at epoch 250 is decoded on fixed template Cercopithecus_diana_208974_CUB. PCA is fitted to 244 training specimen codes; 52 tuning codes are projected. Panels PC1-PC4 each contain 41 actual decoded positions from -2 to +2 training-score SD. Blue indicates inward and orange/red outward displacement along the decoded-mean normal; white is zero. Values are percent of the mean bounding-box diagonal, not reconstruction error or unmapped coverage; tangential motion is not encoded. The symmetric display limit clips the outer 2% of magnitudes. 18 of 164 positions have geometry flags; counts are per sampled surface, not a guarantee for a continuous path or anatomical validity. Templates and confirmation codes are excluded from PCA fitting and score plots. Axes are within-model, not aligned between seeds or datasets. Anatomical interpretation remains provisional.
